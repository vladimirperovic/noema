# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Noema project a respectful, safe, and productive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or preferred technology.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good faith while still discussing technical and security risks clearly.
- Give actionable feedback about code, design, documentation, tests, or deployment behavior rather than attacking people.
- Respect privacy. Do not repost credentials, private URLs, files, screenshots, logs, personal information, or vulnerability details without authorization.
- Credit contributors and sources appropriately.
- Accept maintainers’ decisions about scope, security boundaries, and repository hygiene.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated off-topic promotion.
- Publishing another person’s private information or installation data.
- Publicly disclosing an unpatched vulnerability or active exploit without following the security-reporting process.
- Submitting secrets, stolen data, malicious payloads, or files without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a conduct or security concern.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository code, issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release notes, documentation, security reports, community chats, and project events. It also applies when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, documentation, or other contributions that violate this policy. They may issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant depending on severity and history.

Security reports containing sensitive details may be moved to a private channel. Public copies may be removed to protect users while a fix is prepared.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Report vulnerabilities using the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Include enough context to evaluate the concern, but do not distribute unnecessary private information.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical. Confidentiality will be respected to the extent possible while investigating and resolving the issue.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and adapted for a small self-hosted software project with privacy and security-sensitive user data.
