# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Noema community a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- being respectful and constructive;
- giving and receiving technical feedback in good faith;
- focusing criticism on ideas and code rather than people;
- acknowledging different needs and levels of experience;
- protecting private information;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language;
- personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- publishing another person's private information;
- sharing credentials, private data, or security details without authorization;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces, as well as when someone is publicly representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an appropriate private GitHub channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with respect for confidentiality.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, warnings, comments, issues, or other content that violates this policy. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and common open-source community standards.
